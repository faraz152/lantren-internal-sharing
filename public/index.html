MADE FOR YOUR NETWORK

Just your devices.
Nothing in between.

Lantern Share connects your devices directly over your private local network. Trusted Windows, Mac, and Linux hosts can keep separate room copies. There are no accounts, cloud uploads, analytics, or outside sharing links.

01
Local network only

The server accepts connections from its own Wi-Fi or Ethernet subnet.

02
PIN protected

Devices need the join PIN shown on the host before viewing the shared shelf.

03
Copies stay on your hosts

Paired hosts keep their last room copy. Notes and files move between them only when someone starts a sync.

    MANUAL BACKUP

    Move this sharing room

    For an offline one-time move, create a passphrase-protected backup. Paired hosts can sync directly over your local network instead.

    Download encrypted backup

    Move order: pause and download here, install Lantern Share on the new host, then open its local address and import the backup there. After import, devices use the new host address and the existing room PIN. Keep enough free disk space for the backup and temporary import files.
